# Claude Code Prompt: Transform Chigwell Marquees into a Premium Website

**Goal:** Redesign the Chigwell Marquees website (https://chigwellmarquees.vercel.app/) to achieve a premium, luxurious aesthetic and user experience, drawing inspiration from the North Mymms Park website (https://www.northmymms.co.uk/). The transformation should elevate the brand perception, making it feel more exclusive and sophisticated.

## Key Design Principles to Adopt from North Mymms Park:

1.  **Typography:**
    *   **Headings:** Implement elegant serif fonts for primary headings (e.g., `<h1>`, `<h2>`) to convey a classic and luxurious feel. Examples from North Mymms Park suggest a font similar to a refined Garamond or Playfair Display. Ensure good legibility and appropriate sizing for impact.
    *   **Body Text:** Use clean, highly readable sans-serif fonts for body copy (e.g., `<p>`, `<li>`) that complement the serif headings without clashing. Maintain sufficient line height and letter spacing for a comfortable reading experience.
    *   **Font Pairing:** Focus on a harmonious pairing of one serif and one sans-serif font, using the serif for display/headings and sans-serif for functional text.

2.  **Color Palette:**
    *   **Muted & Earthy Tones:** Shift from the current brighter palette to a more subdued, sophisticated range of colors. Incorporate warm, earthy neutrals (creams, soft greys, taupes) as primary background and text colors.
    *   **Accent Colors:** Introduce subtle, rich accent colors, such as deep forest greens, muted golds, or bronze, for interactive elements, highlights, and key branding elements. Avoid overly bright or saturated colors.
    *   **Contrast:** Ensure sufficient contrast for accessibility while maintaining the muted aesthetic.

3.  **Imagery & Visuals:**
    *   **High-Resolution & Full-Bleed:** Prioritize large, high-resolution images and videos that span the full width of sections, similar to the immersive hero sections and content blocks on North Mymms Park. Images should feel editorial and aspirational.
    *   **Consistent Style:** Apply a consistent visual filter or tone to all images (e.g., slightly desaturated, warm tones, or selective use of black and white/sepia for a timeless effect) to create a cohesive brand identity.
    *   **Strategic Placement:** Use imagery to break up text, create visual interest, and tell a story, rather than just as decorative elements.

4.  **Layout & Structure:**
    *   **Generous Whitespace:** Significantly increase the use of whitespace around content blocks, text, and images. This creates a sense of luxury, allows elements to breathe, and directs user focus.
    *   **Asymmetrical Balance:** Explore more dynamic, less rigid grid layouts. Use asymmetrical arrangements of text and images to create visual interest and a unique flow, while maintaining balance.
    *   **Visual Hierarchy:** Clearly define the hierarchy of information through font sizes, weights, and strategic placement. Important calls to action or headings should stand out elegantly.
    *   **Minimalist Navigation:** Implement a cleaner, possibly more minimalist navigation bar. Consider a subtle sticky header or a hamburger menu for secondary navigation items on desktop, if appropriate, to keep the focus on the main content.

5.  **Interactive Elements & User Experience:**
    *   **Subtle Animations:** Introduce subtle, elegant animations for hover states on buttons and links, and smooth transitions for page scrolls or content loading. Avoid flashy or distracting animations.
    *   **Refined Buttons & Forms:** Redesign buttons and form elements to align with the new color palette and typography. Buttons should have a sophisticated, understated appearance with gentle hover effects.
    *   **Smooth Scrolling:** Ensure a smooth and pleasant scrolling experience throughout the site.

## Specific Areas for Transformation (Chigwell Marquees):

*   **Hero Section:** Redesign the main hero section to feature a full-width, high-resolution image or video background with elegant, centrally aligned serif typography for the main headline and a clear, refined call-to-action button.
*   **Navigation Bar:** Simplify the navigation bar. Consider a more compact logo and a cleaner arrangement of menu items. Integrate contact information subtly.
*   **Content Blocks:** Apply the principles of generous whitespace and elegant typography to all content sections (e.g., 
Weddings, Multicultural Weddings, Corporate & Events). Ensure consistent use of the new color palette and typography.
*   **Image Galleries:** Present image galleries with a more curated, editorial feel. Consider larger image previews and a minimalist lightbox experience.
*   **Footer:** Redesign the footer to be clean and organized, with essential links and contact information presented elegantly.
